Welcome to the ABC Customer Support Knowledge Base. This document contains frequently asked questions about our services, delivery, refunds, memberships, account management, and customer support. Our AI customer support assistant uses this knowledge base to answer customer queries accurately.

==================================================
1. REFUND POLICY
==================================================

Q1. What is the refund policy?

Customers may request a refund within 30 calendar days from the date of delivery if the product is damaged, defective, incorrect, or significantly different from the product description. Refund requests should be submitted through customer support along with the order number and reason for the request.

Q2. How do I request a refund?

You can request a refund by contacting our customer support through email, live chat, or phone. Please provide your order ID, product details, and supporting images if the product is damaged. Our support team will review your request and initiate the refund process.

Q3. How long does it take to receive a refund?

Once the returned product passes inspection, the refund is processed within 5 to 7 business days. The amount is credited to the original payment method. Processing time may vary depending on your bank or payment provider.

Q4. Which products are not eligible for refunds?

Gift cards, downloadable digital products, personalized items, and products damaged due to customer misuse are not eligible for refunds unless required by law.

2. DELIVERY TIMELINES
==================================================

Q1. How long does delivery take?

Orders are usually processed within one business day after payment confirmation.

Estimated delivery times are:
• Local deliveries: 2–4 business days
• Domestic deliveries: 3–7 business days
• Remote locations: 5–10 business days

Q2. Can I track my order?

Yes. Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email and SMS. You can use this number to track your shipment through our website.

Q3. What happens if my delivery is delayed?

Delays may occur because of bad weather, public holidays, transportation issues, or unforeseen circumstances. If your order has not arrived within the expected delivery time, please contact customer support for assistance.

Q4. What if my package is lost?

If the courier confirms that your package has been lost, we will either send a replacement product or issue a full refund after verification.

4. ACCOUNT CANCELLATION
==================================================

Q1. How can I cancel my account?

Customers can cancel their account through the Account Settings page or by contacting customer support.

Q2. What happens after my account is cancelled?

After cancellation, your account will be permanently deleted. Your saved addresses, wishlist, and reward points will be removed. Purchase history required for legal compliance may still be retained.

Q3. Can I recover my account after cancellation?

No. Once the account has been permanently deleted, it cannot be restored. Customers must create a new account if they wish to use our services again.

Q4. Can I stop promotional emails without deleting my account?

Yes. You can unsubscribe from promotional emails through your account settings while keeping your account active.

6. PREMIUM CUSTOMER SUPPORT RULES
==================================================

Q1. Who is eligible for premium customer support?

Premium customer support is available exclusively to Platinum members. Gold members receive priority support, while Silver members receive standard support.

Q2. What services are included in premium support?

Premium support includes:
• 24×7 assistance
• Dedicated support specialists
• Priority ticket handling
• Faster refund verification
• Immediate complaint escalation
• Personalized shopping assistance
• Priority replacement processing

Q3. Do Gold members receive premium support?

No. Gold members receive priority support during business hours, but premium 24×7 support is available only to Platinum members.

Q4. What happens if a customer requests benefits beyond their membership tier?

Customer support representatives will explain the benefits available for the customer's membership tier and cannot promise services reserved for higher membership levels.
